## High-priority open questions

- [CAP-001](accounts/sign-in-with-google.md) When I am already signed in, the sign-in page sends me to whatever address is in its return link without checking it, so a crafted link can bounce a signed-in member to an outside website (an open redirect). The unit test asserts a redirect to an absolute other-domain address, left over from when the sections lived on separate subdomains. Should an already-signed-in visitor be held to the same "this site only" rule as the post-sign-in return? (`apps/mfr/app/sign-in/page.tsx:31-34`, `apps/mfr/app/sign-in/page.test.tsx:101-107`)
- [CAP-002](accounts/new-sign-up-handling.md) A new sign-up whose email matches a shareholder record that an admin has already linked by hand to someone else (e.g. a spouse) takes that record over and is approved automatically; the spouse silently loses access. The code comment says a uniqueness rule prevents this, but that rule only stops one login holding two records. Same policy question as CAP-047's household link. (`apps/mfr/lib/portal/signup.ts:110-120`, `apps/mfr/lib/portal/signup.test.ts:131-136`)
- [CAP-003](accounts/access-for-unapproved-accounts.md) Both pending screens promise "You'll be notified by email once you're approved.", but no email is sent when an admin approves someone. Should an approval email be added, or the promise removed? (`apps/mfr/app/reservations/(public)/pending/page.tsx`, `apps/mfr/app/accounting/pending/page.tsx`, `apps/mfr/app/admin/users/actions.ts:31-59`)
- [CAP-006](portal/home-page.md) The member-only parts of the home page check only that someone is signed in, not that they are approved. A pending or blocked person therefore sees featured family photos, the share figures and event call links. Should these require an approved account like every other member page? (`apps/mfr/app/page.tsx:58-68,160,235`)
- [CAP-011](reunion/meal-rsvps.md) A meal mark is remembered as "day 3 lunch", not as a date or a reunion year. Moving the reunion dates shifts everyone's marks onto different days, and last year's marks carry straight into next year's reunion because nothing clears them. Should marks be tied to actual dates, and cleared or archived each year? (`apps/mfr/lib/db/schema/portal.ts:115-129`, `apps/mfr/lib/portal.ts:122-133`)
- [CAP-014](photos/add-photos.md) Originals are held at public temporary addresses with their location data intact until finalizing succeeds. After a failed save, a closed tab or a timeout, they stay there indefinitely, and nothing sweeps them (`apps/mfr/app/photos/actions.ts:158-160`, `docs/backlog.md:229-245`). That undercuts the on-page promise that "your location data is removed automatically". How long may originals with location data remain, and should they be private?
- [CAP-014](photos/add-photos.md) The step that finishes an upload accepts any web address, not just the member's own just-uploaded original (`z.string().url()`, `apps/mfr/app/photos/actions.ts:112`). The server then fetches that address and deletes it from storage, both after processing fails (`:136-139`) and after it succeeds (`:163`). It looks like an approved member could pass another member's photo address, get a copy under their own name, and break the original. It also lets the server fetch arbitrary addresses. The upload permission step doesn't restrict where the file is placed either (`apps/mfr/app/api/photos/upload-token/route.ts:18-26`). Should finalizing be limited to the member's own temporary upload?
- [CAP-016](photos/delete-photos.md) Both confirmations promise "Backups are kept for 30 days". But a backup exists only where backups are switched on (production) and only if the copy succeeded, so a photo deleted before the nightly backup catches up has none. Restoring is also manual work for an operator; no member or admin can do it (`apps/mfr/app/photos/PhotoGallery.tsx:188, 479`, `apps/mfr/app/photos/actions.ts:227-231`). What should the confirmation promise, and who can ask for a restore?
- [CAP-019](photos/photo-backup-and-recovery.md) Members are told "Backups are kept for 30 days". In practice there is no backup for a photo deleted before its copy succeeded or before the daily check caught up, and restoring is a manual operator procedure with no screen for members or admins (`apps/mfr/app/photos/actions.ts:42-64, 227-231`, `docs/photos-design.md:193-196`). What exactly should members be promised, and who can request a restore?
- [CAP-021](reservations/booking-limits.md) "Today" is worked out in UTC on both the device and the server. In US evenings (from about 7–8 pm Eastern), "today" has already rolled over to tomorrow. A member booking tonight (check-out tomorrow) is refused with "New reservations can't be entirely in the past." The same drift affects "In progress" on My reservations and past/upcoming on the summary. Should "today" follow the cabin's local time zone? (`apps/mfr/lib/reservations/rules/dateHelpers.ts:28-33`)
- [CAP-024](reservations/change-or-cancel-a-reservation.md) A cancelled stay can still have its dates changed outside the booking screens. The change is saved, logged as a change, and the owner is emailed "Reservation updated", yet the stay stays cancelled. Should changes to cancelled stays be refused? (`apps/mfr/app/reservations/api/reservations/[id]/route.ts:34-131`)
- [CAP-024](reservations/change-or-cancel-a-reservation.md) Nothing stops a member cancelling or re-dating a stay that is already over. The calendar's stay details offer "Edit" and "Cancel reservation" on my past stays, and changes are exempt from the past-date rule. Cancelling a finished stay removes it from the summary and frees those nights under the yearly limit, so the limit can be gamed after the fact. Should finished stays be locked for members? (`apps/mfr/app/reservations/(app)/calendar/ReservationViewDialog.tsx:72-87`; `apps/mfr/app/reservations/api/reservations/[id]/route.ts:34-147`; `apps/mfr/lib/reservations/rules/validateReservation.ts:97`)
- [CAP-026](reservations/blocked-dates.md) The "End date" of a block is not blocked: a block of October 1 to October 10 closes the nights of October 1–9, and October 10 stays bookable as a check-in. The form doesn't say this. Blocking a single day needs an end date of the next day, because the same day is refused with "endDate must be after startDate". Should the end date be inclusive, or at least be relabelled (e.g. "First day open again")? (`apps/mfr/app/reservations/(app)/admin/(reservations-admin)/blocked-dates/BlockedDatesClient.tsx:100-108`; `apps/mfr/lib/db/push-only.sql:13-29`)
- [CAP-027](reservations/booking-rules-settings.md) On a new environment with no rules saved yet, this page shows "Failed to load booking rules." and has no form. The calendar tells members to "configure booking rules under Admin → Booking Rules", which this page can't do, so the first-time setup can only happen outside the app. Separately, the server accepts any values it is sent (zero or negative limits, fractions, any horizon text); only the form's minimum of 1 protects it. Should the page offer first-time setup, and should the server reject bad values? (`apps/mfr/app/reservations/(app)/admin/(reservations-admin)/booking-rules/BookingRulesClient.tsx:27-36`; `apps/mfr/app/reservations/api/admin/booking-rules/route.ts:31-56`)
- [CAP-036](accounting/transaction-corrections.md) A transaction can be corrected twice, and both corrections count. The server never checks whether the original was already corrected. The correction form still opens for an already-corrected transaction from a direct link or from "Issue correction" on its dispute. (`apps/mfr/actions/accounting/corrections.ts:51-57`, `apps/mfr/app/accounting/admin/transactions/transactions-client.tsx:239-244`, `apps/mfr/app/accounting/admin/activity/page.tsx:166-173`)
- [CAP-036](accounting/transaction-corrections.md) Accepting the default amounts reverses a transaction twice. The form says "Amounts default to a full reversal" and pre-fills the negative of each original amount. But the original is also marked corrected and stops counting, so the correction's amounts _replace_ it rather than cancel it. Worked example (the e2e scenario): Anna Miller buys 250; 100 is transferred to Ben Miller; the transfer is corrected with the defaults (+100 for Member, −100 for Transferee). Balances end at Member 350 / Transferee −100, not 250 / 0. The e2e test comments claim 250 / 0, but its checks are too loose to notice. Also, since zero amounts are refused, there is no way to fully void a mistaken transaction. Should the defaults be the original amounts, or should corrections go back to being deltas? (`apps/mfr/app/accounting/admin/transactions/transactions-client.tsx:374-398`, `apps/mfr/actions/accounting/corrections.ts:62-117`, `e2e/tests/accounting-flows.spec.ts:103-127,180-194`; the soft-delete behaviour was added in commit 8826e8c)
- [CAP-041](accounting/share-purchase-goal.md) A correction's own amount never counts toward "raised this year", the goal, "Shares sold by year" or a shareholder's "Purchased" figure, because those count only share purchases and a correction is a different kind of entry. So fixing a 100-share purchase down to 90 removes all 100 from those figures while balances correctly show 90. Should a corrected share purchase still count as a share purchase? (`apps/mfr/lib/accounting/ledger.ts:13-29,135-156`, `apps/mfr/actions/accounting/corrections.ts:84`)
- [CAP-044](accounting/expense-log.md) Edits and deletes leave no audit trail. An edit overwrites the amount, category and description in place without recording who changed it or what it was before. A delete removes the expense permanently. The activity log only shows when an expense was first entered, with its current values. Should changes and deletions to expenses (e.g. a tax payment) be logged, and should deletes be reversible like ledger corrections? (`apps/mfr/actions/accounting/expenses.ts:92-101`, `:109-129`; `apps/mfr/app/accounting/admin/activity/page.tsx:49-76`)
- [CAP-046](accounting/shareholder-records.md) A shareholder who still holds shares can be deactivated with one click, with no warning or confirmation. They then drop off the ownership list while the total still counts their shares, so the listed percentages no longer add up to 100%. Their shares also can't be transferred out, because transfers need both shareholders to be active. Should deactivating a shareholder with a non-zero balance be blocked, or at least confirmed? (`apps/mfr/lib/accounting/ledger.ts:49-77`; `apps/mfr/actions/accounting/transfers.ts:63`; `apps/mfr/app/accounting/admin/shareholders/[id]/edit/edit-shareholder-form.tsx:55-69`)
- [CAP-047](accounting/link-logins-to-shareholders.md) "Unlink" doesn't hold when the login's email matches the shareholder's email: the next visit links it straight back. The only way to remove that person's access is to change the shareholder's email. Unlink also asks for no confirmation. Should unlinking stick, and should it be confirmed? (`apps/mfr/actions/accounting/shareholders.ts:257-262` vs `apps/mfr/lib/auth.ts:47-70`; `apps/mfr/app/accounting/admin/shareholders/[id]/edit/link-login-control.tsx:124-129`)
- [CAP-047](accounting/link-logins-to-shareholders.md) A household's link made by hand can be taken over. If "John and Michele Anderegg" carries John's email and an admin linked Michele's login, John's next sign-in (or first sign-up) re-points the record to John. Michele then loses the household dashboard with no warning to anyone. A code comment in the sign-up handler says the database prevents this; it doesn't. Should a record carrying one spouse's email be able to keep the other spouse linked, and should an existing link ever be replaced automatically? (`apps/mfr/lib/auth.ts:65-70`; `apps/mfr/lib/portal/signup.ts:111-116`)
